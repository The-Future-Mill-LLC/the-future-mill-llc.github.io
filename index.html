The 405

The world's slowest racing game. For iPhone.

A comic driving and traffic-jam puzzle game set on Los Angeles's most famous freeway. Your car speeds up on its own: you steer, brake and honk. Hit the traffic and the road turns into a sliding-car puzzle. Shuffle the stuck cars until yours can drive out, then get back on the road.

What's on the road

  • 100 levels on the real 405, from Sylmar to Irvine, southbound or northbound
  • Self-driving sleepers, lowriders, lifted trucks, lane-splitting motorcycles and road ragers
  • Dawn, rush hour and late night, with June gloom fog, Santa Ana winds and the odd drop of rain
  • Seven speed runs with Game Center leaderboards
  • Driving and puzzle difficulty set separately, from ages 4 and up to 120 mph
  • Free to play with ads; optional Ad-Free, 405 Live and tokens
